# Mandatory fourth route: TEM falsification

The fixed cumulative command downloads and authenticates both primary archives, checks every ZIP path before extraction, records per-member SHA-256 digests, identifies CPU executable candidates, and performs bounded `--help` capability probes. A negative control flips exactly one byte in the pinned simulator archive and requires the identity verifier to reject it.

The independent checker first restates the exact claim logic, protocol, domains, and assumptions. It then requires the two published identities, safe nonempty archives, explicit CPU/no-GPU allocation, four distinct Claim 6 routes, and the corruption control. It rejects falsification unless all assumptions are satisfied and a genuine contradiction is found.

This is a provenance, capability, and falsification audit—not a proxy TEM optimization.
