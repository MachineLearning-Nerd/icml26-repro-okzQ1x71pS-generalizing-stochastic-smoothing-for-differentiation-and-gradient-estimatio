# Claim 6 limitations and deviations

- The authors' application implementation is not publicly linked in the paper or discoverable from the cited project pages. This campaign reconstructs disclosed protocols from the paper and cited primary repositories.
- This first MNIST route times 100 steps and does not run 100,000 steps or 12 seeds. It must remain BLOCKED regardless of its accuracy or loss.
- The paper does not identify the cross-validated gamma or exact variance-reduction cell for each MNIST table entry. This calibration uses the disclosed candidate gamma 1/3 and the paper's stated generally preferred randomized Latin/LOO combination.
- Torchvision's 60,000-image training split is divided deterministically into 55,000 train and 5,000 validation images to match the cited NeuralSort cardinalities.
- The Warcraft route executes only two warm-up and 20 measured optimizer steps, not 50 epochs or five seeds. Its test metric is diagnostic and cannot verify the paper's reported accuracy.
- The paper does not identify the Figure 6 Warcraft gamma or the precise output-to-positive-cost transform. This route fixes gamma 0.1 from the disclosed Figure 7 sweep and uses log vertex costs; both are explicit reconstruction choices.
- The first Warcraft verifier required byte-for-byte path-label identity and rejected three of 64 independently reconstructed paths. A second verifier also rejected those three because it demanded exact costs after the official weights had been serialized as `float16`. The current verifier supersedes both with path validity and overlap of independent cost intervals induced by half-precision quantization, while retaining exact encoding and exact stored-cost agreement as diagnostics.
- The rendering route executes a pinned-source capability test, not pose optimization. The cited source is CUDA-only and differs from the paper protocol, and GPU use is prohibited. No substitute renderer is accepted as evidence.
- The TEM route authenticates and inspects the public simulator and TMV example packages on CPU, but does not execute the paper's optimization. The exact input deck, specimen mapping, smoothing implementation, initializations, Adam learning rate, and horizon are not disclosed. Missing capability is not treated as falsification of an existential demonstration.
